About TVRC

Welcome to the Trauma Video Review Collaborative. Here you can find templates and resources for trauma video review. Please feel free to download and use the templates provided. You can also search and directly import the TVRC Data Dictionary in REDCap using the instrument search function.

For general inquiries, contact us at: TVRCollaborative@proton.me
